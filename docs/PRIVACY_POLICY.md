# Royal Ludo 3D Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 28 November 2026

Royal Ludo 3D ("the App") is a casual board-game app operated by the app publisher. This policy explains the information processed when you use the App.

## Information the App may process
- **Account and gameplay:** Firebase Authentication identifiers (including anonymous sign-in), game room identifiers, player moves, match events and basic game state needed to operate online games.
- **Device and diagnostics:** app version, device/OS information, crash and performance diagnostics where provided by Google/Firebase services.
- **Advertising:** when production Google Mobile Ads is enabled, Google may process advertising identifiers, IP address, app interactions and related signals to deliver, measure and prevent invalid ad traffic. The publisher does not receive a user's full advertising profile.
- **Support messages:** information you voluntarily send to the publisher for help.

## How information is used
Information is used to authenticate sessions, run and secure matches, prevent cheating and abuse, maintain service reliability, respond to support requests and (where enabled) display and measure ads.

## Sharing
The App uses Google Firebase services for authentication and online game infrastructure. If ads are enabled, Google Mobile Ads processes information under Google's own terms and privacy disclosures. The publisher does not sell personal information. Data may be disclosed when legally required or to protect users and the service.

## Children
The App is not specifically directed to children under the age required by applicable law. The publisher must complete Google Play's target-audience and Families declarations accurately before release. If the app is configured for children, child-directed ad and data rules must be applied before publishing.

## Retention and deletion
Online game data is retained as needed to operate, secure and troubleshoot the service. Users may request deletion of account-associated data by contacting the publisher at the support email published on the Google Play listing. Anonymous accounts may not have a recoverable email address; provide the in-app user identifier when requesting deletion.

## Security
The publisher uses Firebase security rules and server-side game operations to limit unauthorized modification. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

## Your choices
You can stop using the App at any time. Device advertising and privacy controls are available through Android and Google's settings. Availability of personalized ads depends on location, consent requirements and the publisher's AdMob configuration.

## Changes and contact
This policy may be updated when app features or service providers change. Before release, the publisher must replace the support contact placeholder on the Play listing with a monitored email address.

**Publisher contact:** Add a monitored support email before publication.
